Everything it takes to run an event

Grouped by what you're actually doing that day: capturing the booking, selling and confirming it, then delivering it and getting paid.

Get started

Capture the booking

Get the enquiry into a system, not a notebook

An enquiry becomes a customer record and a calendar hold, so nothing depends on someone remembering a WhatsApp message.

01 — Customers

A lightweight CRM built for events

  • Every contact keeps its full event history in one place
  • Notes and preferences travel with the customer, not a sticky note
  • Follow-up reminders surface automatically when a lead goes quiet
  • Search by name, phone or past event to find anyone in seconds
02 — Event calendar

See every booking at a glance

  • Day, week and month views switch with one click
  • Bookings colour-code by status: enquiry, confirmed, paid, complete
  • Double-booking warnings catch a room or date clash before you confirm
  • Filter by room, staff member or event type to see just what you need
Sell & confirm

Turn interest into a signed, paid booking

A proposal the client can accept online, converting straight into a contract and invoice without retyping a line.

03 — Proposals

Branded proposals clients accept online

  • Menus, packages and rental items pull from your own catalogue
  • Headcount and pricing recalculate automatically as details change
  • Clients review and accept from their phone, no printing required
  • Every version sent is saved, so nothing gets lost in an email thread
04 — Contracts & invoices

One click turns a proposal into paperwork

  • One click turns an accepted proposal into a contract and invoice
  • Deposit, payment and balance stay visible on the same record
  • Terms and cancellation policy attach automatically from your templates
  • Overdue balances are easy to spot at a glance
Deliver & get paid

Run the event and close out the record

Your floor and kitchen work from one sheet, and the money side stays visible from deposit to final balance.

05 — Payments

Deposits and balances, taken on the booking

  • Take a deposit at booking and the balance closer to the date
  • Card on file means the balance can be charged without a new form
  • Refunds and adjustments log straight to the customer record
  • A running total shows what's paid, pending and owed across your calendar
06 — Banquet Event Orders

A one-sheet your floor and kitchen trust

  • One printable sheet: timing, headcount, menu, layout, staffing
  • Built from the same booking data as the contract, so it can't drift
  • Dietary notes and allergies sit next to the menu, not a separate email
  • Update the booking and the BEO updates with it
Included & add-on

What comes with every module

A few capabilities that run across the whole app rather than living in one feature.

Built-in e-signature

Track sent, opened and signed status on every contract, with no per-signature fee.

Multi-language documents

Proposals and contracts send in English (default), Igbo, Yoruba or Hausa.

24/7 online booking form

A public enquiry form that lands directly as a real event on your calendar, any hour.

AI assistant

Drafts follow-up emails and sends automatic status notifications to clients and staff.
