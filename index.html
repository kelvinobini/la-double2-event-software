FAQ

Questions teams ask before switching

Who is LA Double2 built for?

Small to mid-size caterers, banquet halls, event centres, hotel event teams, and restaurants and clubs that host private functions.

Do I need to install anything?

No. LA Double2 runs in your browser on desktop, tablet and phone. There is nothing to install and nothing to maintain on your side.

What does the AI assistant do?

It reviews your open bookings and drafts follow-up emails, flags leads that have gone quiet, and sends automatic status notifications to clients and staff. You approve or edit everything it drafts before it sends.

What is a BEO?

A Banquet Event Order is the one-sheet your kitchen and floor staff work from: timing, headcount, menu, room layout, dietary notes and staffing, generated from the same booking data as the proposal and invoice.

Does this work for off-premise catering, not just on-site venues?

Yes. Off-premise jobs use the same customer, proposal, contract and BEO flow as on-site events; you just skip the room and layout fields that don't apply.

How is this different from enterprise event platforms?

LA Double2 stays deliberately light: no per-signature or per-document fees, and a setup you can finish yourself in minutes rather than a sales-led rollout.

Is e-signature included?

Yes, with no per-signature charge. You can see when a contract was sent, opened and signed.

What languages are supported?

The interface and client-facing documents are available in English (the default), Igbo, Yoruba and Hausa.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Customers, events, proposals, contracts and invoices export to CSV or PDF at any time.
